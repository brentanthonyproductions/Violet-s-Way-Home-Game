    Violet's Way Home

    Violet chased a moth out the front door and wandered too far. Lead her back across the backyard, the park, Main Street and Willow Lane, then clear out the house to reach her blue blanket.

    Swat enemiesDogs, crows, spray bottles and roombas. Each one gives XP.
    Grab toysYarn, mice, feathers and bells give XP. Catnip heals.
    Make friendsTalk to the 6 neighborhood cats. Each friend levels Violet up twice.

    Toys, catnip and swatted enemies fill the purple Power bar. When it's full, right-click for the Calico Cyclone, a spinning attack that hits everything around Violet. Blue blankets are nap spots. Step on one to heal and save your progress. The yellow arrow at the top always points home.

    Best played with a keyboard and mouse.
